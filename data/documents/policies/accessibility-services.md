# Accessibility Services

The library is committed to equal access for all users.

- All branches have step-free entrances and lifts to every floor.
- Two accessible study rooms with adjustable desks are on the ground floor of the Main Library and can be booked for up to 4 hours a day.
- Library computers include screen reader (NVDA) and magnification software.
- Students registered with the Disability Support Office get extended loans (double the normal period), higher hold limits, and a book fetching service: staff will retrieve books from the shelves within 1 working day.
- Alternative formats (accessible PDF, large print) can be requested for course materials.

Contact accessibility@northbridge-library.example to register for these services.
