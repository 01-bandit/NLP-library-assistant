# Citation and Referencing Guide

Northbridge departments use different citation styles: Computing and Engineering use IEEE, Social Sciences and Psychology use APA 7th edition, Law uses OSCOLA, and Humanities use Chicago or MLA. Always check with your instructor.

## APA example (book)
Kahneman, D. (2011). Thinking, fast and slow. Farrar, Straus and Giroux.

## IEEE example (book)
[1] R. C. Martin, Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship. Upper Saddle River, NJ, USA: Prentice Hall, 2008.

The library recommends Zotero, a free reference manager, which is installed on all library computers. Help with referencing is available at the Research Help desk.
