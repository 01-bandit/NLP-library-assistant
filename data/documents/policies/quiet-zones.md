# Study Spaces and Quiet Zones

## Silent zone
Floor 3 of the Main Library is a silent zone. Phone calls, conversation and group work are not allowed. Phones must be on silent.

## Quiet zone
Floors 1 and 2 are quiet zones where brief, low-volume conversation is allowed.

## Collaborative zone
The ground floor Learning Commons and Floor 4 group study rooms are collaborative spaces where group discussion is welcome.

## Reserving seats
Individual desks cannot be reserved. Belongings left unattended for more than 30 minutes may be removed by staff and taken to Lost and Found so that seats are available for others.
