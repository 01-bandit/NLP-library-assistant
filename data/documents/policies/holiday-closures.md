# Holiday Closures

All branches are closed on national public holidays, including Pakistan Day (23 March), Labour Day (1 May), Independence Day (14 August), Iqbal Day (9 November) and Quaid-e-Azam Day (25 December), and for the Eid holidays as announced by the university.

During the semester break, the Main Library runs reduced hours: Monday to Friday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, closed at weekends. The Science and Law Libraries are closed during the semester break.

Due dates that fall on a closure day are automatically moved to the next opening day, and no fines are charged for closure days. Closure announcements are posted on the library website and social media at least one week in advance.
