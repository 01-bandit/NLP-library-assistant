# Library Code of Conduct

All visitors must respect the library, its collections and other users.

- Show a valid ID card or day pass when asked by staff.
- Keep noise to the level allowed in each zone.
- Do not mark, cut or damage library materials.
- Do not reserve seats with belongings for long periods.
- Smoking and vaping are prohibited throughout the building.
- Covered drinks are allowed on all floors except Special Collections; hot food is only allowed in the ground floor café area.
- Harassment or abusive behaviour toward staff or other users is not tolerated.

Breaking the code of conduct may lead to a warning, removal from the building, or suspension of library privileges for up to one semester, decided by the Head of Library Services.
