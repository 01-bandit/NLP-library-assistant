# Laptop and Equipment Loans

Students can borrow laptops, phone chargers, calculators and headphones from the circulation desk of the Main Library.

## Laptops
Laptops are lent for 4 hours for use inside the library only. Each student may borrow one laptop at a time and may not borrow another laptop on the same day after returning one if others are waiting. Laptops must be returned at least 30 minutes before closing.

## Other equipment
Chargers, calculators and headphones are lent for 4 hours.

## Fines
Late laptops are fined at PKR 500 per day; lost or damaged laptops are charged at full replacement cost. A student who returns a laptop late twice in a semester loses laptop borrowing privileges for the rest of that semester.
