# Interlibrary Loan (ILL)

If Northbridge does not own a book or article you need, you can request it from another library through Interlibrary Loan.

## Who can use ILL
Postgraduate students and faculty may request ILL items free of charge. Undergraduate students may request up to 5 ILL items per semester at PKR 300 per book; articles are free.

## How long it takes
Articles usually arrive by email as PDF within 3 to 5 working days. Physical books take 2 to 3 weeks.

## Loan rules
The lending library sets the loan period, which is typically 21 days. ILL books must be collected and returned at the Main Library circulation desk. The overdue fine for ILL books is PKR 100 per day, and lost ILL items are charged at the replacement cost set by the lending library.

Submit requests through the ILL form on the library website.
