# Overdue Notices and Escalation

The library sends automated reminders to your university email address:

- 3 days before the due date: courtesy reminder.
- On the due date: due today notice.
- 1, 7 and 14 days after the due date: overdue notices.
- 30 days after the due date: the item is declared lost and the replacement charge is added to your account.

At 45 days overdue, unresolved accounts are reported to the Registrar's Office, which may place a hold on course registration. Not receiving a reminder does not excuse an overdue fine, so please keep your contact details up to date in My Account.
