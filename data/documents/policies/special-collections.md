# Special Collections and Archives

Special Collections holds rare books, manuscripts, historical maps, and the university archive. It is located on Floor 5 of the Main Library and is open Monday to Thursday, 10:00 am to 4:00 pm, by appointment only.

Rules:
- Book an appointment at least 2 working days in advance.
- Bags, food and drinks are not allowed; lockers are provided.
- Only pencils may be used for notes.
- Photography without flash is allowed for personal research; publication requires written permission.

Special Collections items never leave the reading room. Digitised items are available in the Digital Collections portal.
