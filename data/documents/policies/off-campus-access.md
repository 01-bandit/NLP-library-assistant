# Off-Campus Access to E-Resources

Current students and staff can access licensed e-resources from off campus.

1. Go to the library website and use the Discover search or the A to Z database list.
2. When prompted, sign in with your university email and password (single sign-on).
3. If a database still asks you to pay, check that you started from the library website and not from Google.

The university VPN is not needed for most databases. If you see "access denied", clear your browser cookies and try again, then contact eresources@northbridge-library.example with a screenshot and the link you used.
