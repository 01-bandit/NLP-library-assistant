# Workshops and Training

The library runs free workshops each semester. Registration is required through the Events page; places are limited to 25 per session.

- Library Orientation: weekly in the first 3 weeks of each semester, 45 minutes.
- Finding Journal Articles: databases and search strategies, 1 hour.
- Referencing with Zotero: managing references and generating citations in APA, IEEE and Harvard styles, 1.5 hours.
- Avoiding Plagiarism and Using Turnitin: 1 hour.
- Systematic Literature Reviews for postgraduates: 2 hours.

Attendance certificates are emailed to participants who attend the full session.
