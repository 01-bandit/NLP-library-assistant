# Holds and Reservations

If every copy of a book is on loan, you can place a hold (reservation) on it through the online catalogue or at the circulation desk. Each member can have up to 5 active holds.

When a hold is placed, the current borrower's loan is shortened: they are recalled and must return the item within 7 days or by the original due date, whichever is earlier. Recalled items cannot be renewed.

When the item comes back, you receive an email and an SMS. Held items are kept on the Holds Shelf beside the circulation desk for 5 days. If you do not collect it within 5 days, the hold passes to the next person in the queue.

Holds are free. Cancelling a hold is also free and can be done from My Account.
