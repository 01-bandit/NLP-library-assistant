# Lost and Found

Items found in the library are taken to the circulation desk of the branch where they were found. They are kept for 30 days, after which unclaimed items are donated or disposed of. University ID cards are sent to Student Services after 3 days, and valuables such as phones, wallets and laptops are handed to Campus Security on the same day.

To claim an item, describe it and show your ID at the circulation desk. The library is not responsible for personal belongings left unattended.
