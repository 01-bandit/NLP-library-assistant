# Course Reserves

Course Reserves are books and readings that instructors set aside for a specific course so that every student has access.

Reserve items are kept behind the circulation desk in the Main Library and Science Library. They are lent for 3 hours for use inside the library only. A maximum of 2 Reserve items may be borrowed at a time. Reserve items cannot be renewed back-to-back if another student is waiting.

The overdue fine for Course Reserve items is PKR 100 per hour or part of an hour.

To find a reserve item, search the catalogue by course code (for example, CS 201) or instructor name. Faculty can submit reserve requests through the Course Reserves form at least 7 days before the start of the semester.
