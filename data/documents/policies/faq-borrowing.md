# Frequently Asked Questions: Borrowing and Fines

How many books can I borrow as an undergraduate? Up to 3 books at a time for 14 days.

Can I renew a book twice? Students can renew only once, and only if nobody has placed a hold. Faculty can renew up to three times.

What happens if I return a book late? You are charged PKR 20 per day for standard loans after a one-day grace period, up to PKR 1,500 per book.

Why can't I borrow anything? Your account is probably blocked because unpaid fines are PKR 1,000 or more, or because an item is more than 30 days overdue. Pay the fine or return the item to restore borrowing.

Can someone else return my books? Yes, anyone can return items on your behalf at the desk or book drop.

Can I borrow books from the Law Library if I am not a law student? Yes, all members can borrow from any branch, but some law reports are reference-only.
