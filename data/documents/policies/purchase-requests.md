# Purchase Requests

Students and staff can suggest books, e-books or journals for the library to buy using the Purchase Suggestion form on the library website.

Requests are reviewed weekly by the subject librarians. Priority is given to items needed for current courses and research. You will receive an email decision within 14 days. If approved, print books typically arrive within 4 to 6 weeks, and a hold is automatically placed for the person who requested it. E-books are usually available within 1 week.

Each member may submit up to 10 suggestions per semester.
