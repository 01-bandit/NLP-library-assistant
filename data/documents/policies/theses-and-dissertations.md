# Theses and Dissertations

Copies of Northbridge master's and PhD theses since 1995 are held in the Main Library, Floor 1, Theses Room. Theses are reference-only and cannot be borrowed. Photocopying is limited to 10% of a thesis.

Theses submitted since 2015 are available full-text in the Northbridge Research Repository unless the author requested an embargo, which can last up to 2 years.

## Submitting your thesis
Postgraduate students must deposit one hardbound copy and one PDF with the library after final approval. The library's clearance certificate, required for degree award, is issued only after the deposit and after all fines are cleared.
