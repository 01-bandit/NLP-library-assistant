# Returning Items

Items can be returned to any Northbridge branch, regardless of where they were borrowed.

## During opening hours
Return items at the circulation desk or at the self-return machines near the entrance of each branch. You receive an emailed receipt.

## Book drop
The 24-hour book drop is outside the Main Library entrance. Items placed in the book drop are checked in the next morning and backdated to the previous opening day, so no fine is charged for the night.

## What cannot go in the book drop
Laptops, chargers, equipment, Course Reserve items and interlibrary loan items must be returned at the circulation desk in person, because they can be damaged in the drop box.
