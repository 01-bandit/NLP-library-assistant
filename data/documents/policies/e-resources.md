# E-Resources and Databases

The library subscribes to more than 60 online databases and over 200,000 e-books. Key databases include IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, JSTOR, SpringerLink, PubMed, Westlaw and the HEC National Digital Library.

E-resources are available to current students and staff only. Alumni, visitors and external researchers can use most databases only from library computers.

To search all resources at once, use the "Discover" search box on the library website. Downloading an entire e-book or using automated scripts to bulk-download articles breaks licence terms and may lead to the university losing access, so systematic downloading is prohibited.
