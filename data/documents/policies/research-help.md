# Research Help and Subject Librarians

Subject librarians help with literature searches, choosing databases, systematic reviews and research data management.

You can book a 30-minute one-to-one appointment with a subject librarian through the library website. Drop-in help is available at the Research Help desk in the Main Library, Monday to Thursday, 11:00 am to 3:00 pm.

Quick questions can also be sent through the Ask a Librarian chat during opening hours.
