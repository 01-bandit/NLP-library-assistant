# Computers and Wi-Fi

The Main Library has 80 public computers in the Learning Commons and the Science Library has 40. Computers have Microsoft Office, SPSS, MATLAB, R, Python and Zotero installed. Log in with your university account. Day pass visitors receive a guest login valid for 2 hours.

## Wi-Fi
Students and staff connect to the "Northbridge-Secure" network with their university credentials. Visitors can use "Northbridge-Guest", which requires a code printed on the day pass and is limited to 4 hours per day.

Saving files to the computers is not permanent; files are deleted at logout. Save your work to OneDrive or a USB drive.
