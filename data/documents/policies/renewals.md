# Renewals

Books can be renewed once if nobody else has placed a hold on them. A renewal extends the loan by the same length as the original loan period, starting from the renewal date.

You can renew online through My Account on the library website, at the self-checkout kiosks, or by asking at the circulation desk. Renewals are not possible by email.

Items cannot be renewed if they are already overdue by more than 7 days, if your account is blocked because of unpaid fines of PKR 1,000 or more, or if the item is a Short Loan or Course Reserve item.

Faculty members may renew up to three times. Interlibrary loan items can only be renewed if the lending library agrees; request this at least 3 days before the due date.
