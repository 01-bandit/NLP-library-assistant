# Frequently Asked Questions: General

Can I bring a friend who is not a student? Yes, they can buy a day pass for PKR 200 at the entrance with a CNIC or passport.

Can I eat in the library? Hot food is only allowed in the ground floor café area. Covered drinks are allowed on all floors except Special Collections.

Is there a place to charge my phone? Yes, charging points are at every desk on Floors 1 to 3, and phone chargers can be borrowed from the circulation desk.

Do you have lockers? Yes, day-use lockers are on the ground floor. They are free and must be emptied by closing time; contents left overnight are removed.

Is parking available? The library does not manage parking; use the university visitor parking near Gate 2.
