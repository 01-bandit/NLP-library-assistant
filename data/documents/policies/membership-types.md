# Membership Types

## Students
All enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students are library members automatically. Your university ID card is your library card.

## Faculty and staff
Academic and administrative staff are members automatically. Faculty receive extended loan limits and may request books for Course Reserves.

## Alumni
Graduates of Northbridge University can buy an alumni membership for PKR 3,000 per year. Alumni may borrow up to 2 books for 14 days and use the reading rooms, but cannot access licensed e-resources from off campus.

## External researchers
Researchers from other institutions can apply for a reference-only membership for PKR 5,000 per semester with a letter from their institution. They may use materials inside the library but cannot borrow.

## Visitors
Members of the public can buy a day pass for PKR 200 at the main entrance with a CNIC or passport. Day pass holders can use the reading rooms and public computers but cannot borrow.
