# Printing, Copying and Scanning

Printers and photocopiers are available on every floor of the Main Library and on Floor 1 of the Science Library.

## Prices
- Black and white A4: PKR 5 per page
- Colour A4: PKR 20 per page
- A3 black and white: PKR 10 per page
- Scanning to email or USB: free

Print credit is topped up at the circulation desk or through the university payment portal. Every student receives 100 free black and white pages at the start of each semester.

## Copyright
You may copy up to one chapter or 10% of a book, whichever is greater, for personal study. Copying entire books is not permitted.
