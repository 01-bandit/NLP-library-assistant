# Fines and Fees

Overdue fines are charged per item, per day, including weekends and public holidays when the library is open.

## Overdue fines
- Standard loan books: PKR 20 per day.
- Short loan items (3-day loans): PKR 50 per day.
- Course Reserve items (3-hour loans): PKR 100 per hour or part of an hour.
- Laptops and equipment: PKR 500 per day.
- Recalled items not returned by the new due date: PKR 100 per day.

## Maximum fine
The overdue fine for a single book is capped at PKR 1,500. After 30 days overdue the item is treated as lost (see Lost and Damaged Items).

## Grace period
There is a one-day grace period for standard loan books only: a book returned the day after its due date is not fined.

## Account blocks
Borrowing privileges are suspended when unpaid fines reach PKR 1,000 or more. Privileges are restored immediately after payment.
