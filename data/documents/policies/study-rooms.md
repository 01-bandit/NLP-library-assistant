# Group Study Rooms

The Main Library has 12 bookable group study rooms on Floor 4, and the Science Library has 6 rooms on Floor 3. Each room seats 4 to 8 people and has a whiteboard and a wall-mounted screen with HDMI.

## Booking rules
- Rooms are booked online through the Room Booking system using your university login.
- Bookings are available for groups of 3 or more students.
- Each student may book up to 2 hours per day and 6 hours per week.
- Bookings can be made up to 7 days in advance.
- If nobody checks in within 15 minutes of the start time, the booking is released.

Rooms are free to use. Food is not allowed in study rooms; covered drinks are permitted.
