# Borrowing Policy

This policy applies to all branches of Northbridge University Library: the Main Library, the Science Library and the Law Library.

## Loan limits and loan periods
- Undergraduate students: up to 3 books at a time, for 14 days.
- Postgraduate students: up to 8 books at a time, for 28 days.
- Faculty and academic staff: up to 20 books at a time, for a full semester (due on the last day of the semester).
- Alumni members: up to 2 books at a time, for 14 days.
- Visitors with a day pass: cannot borrow items.

Short loan items are lent for 3 days, and Course Reserve items for 3 hours (see Course Reserves).

## What you need
A valid university ID card or digital library card is required for every loan. Items are checked out at the circulation desk or at the self-checkout kiosks on each floor.

## Non-loanable items
Reference books, journals, newspapers, theses and Special Collections material cannot be taken out of the library.
