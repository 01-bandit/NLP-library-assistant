# Book Donations

The library welcomes donations of books that fit the university's teaching and research areas. Before bringing donations, email collections@northbridge-library.example with a list of titles.

The library does not accept textbooks more than 10 years old (except classic works), damaged or annotated books, magazines, photocopies, or encyclopedia sets.

Donated items become the property of the library. Items that are not added to the collection may be offered to students in the annual book sale or given to other libraries. Donors of 50 or more accepted items can request a donation acknowledgement letter.
