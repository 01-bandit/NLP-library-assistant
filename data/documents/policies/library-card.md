# Library Card and My Account

For students and staff, the university ID card works as the library card. A digital library card is available in the Northbridge mobile app and is accepted at self-checkout kiosks and the circulation desk.

## Lost card
Report a lost ID card to the Student Services office. A temporary library barcode can be issued at the circulation desk for up to 14 days while you wait for a replacement.

## My Account
Log in to My Account on the library website with your university email and password to see your loans, due dates, holds, fines and borrowing history. You can renew items, cancel holds and pay fines there.

The library assistant chatbot cannot see or change your account. For account-specific questions contact the circulation desk.
