# Paying Fines

Fines can be paid at the circulation desk in any branch by cash or debit card, online through My Account using the university payment portal, or added to your student fee voucher at the end of the semester.

Receipts are emailed automatically. The library cannot accept payment by bank transfer to individual staff members.

## Fine appeals
If you believe a fine was charged in error (for example, the library was closed, you were hospitalised, or you returned the item to the book drop on time), submit an appeal form within 30 days of the fine being charged. Appeals are reviewed by the Circulation Manager within 10 working days. Fines remain on your account but do not count towards the PKR 1,000 block limit while an appeal is pending.

Fines must be cleared before graduation; unpaid library fines will block the issue of your degree transcript.
