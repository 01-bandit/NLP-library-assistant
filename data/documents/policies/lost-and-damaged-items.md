# Lost and Damaged Items

Report a lost item as soon as possible at the circulation desk or by email to circulation@northbridge-library.example. Overdue fines stop accruing from the date you report the loss.

## Charges for lost items
You are charged the current replacement cost of the item plus a non-refundable processing fee of PKR 750. If the replacement cost cannot be determined, a standard charge of PKR 4,000 applies for books and PKR 1,500 for other media.

## Found it later?
If you find and return the item within 60 days of paying for it, the replacement cost is refunded, but the processing fee and any overdue fines are not.

## Damage
Water damage, torn or missing pages, heavy highlighting, and damaged barcodes are treated as damage. Minor damage is charged at PKR 300 for repair. Items that cannot be repaired are charged as lost. Please do not attempt to repair library books yourself.
