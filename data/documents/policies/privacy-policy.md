# Library Privacy Policy

The library keeps borrowing records only for as long as needed to manage loans. Your borrowing history is visible only to you in My Account. Records of returned items are anonymised after 12 months unless there is an unresolved fine.

Library staff do not share what you borrow or search for with instructors, other students or third parties, except when required by law or university disciplinary procedures.

The library assistant chatbot does not store personal account data and cannot access your loans or fines. Do not share your password or ID number in the chat.
